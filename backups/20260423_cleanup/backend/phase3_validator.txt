VALIDATION RULES:

### Rule 1: CEILING_AREA_MATCHES_FLOOR

**Check:** Lubų plotas (ceiling area) should be 95-105% of total floor area (excluding garage).

**Why:** A house has roughly the same ceiling area as floor area. Deviation >10% means either:
- Garage included in wrong place
- Missing rooms in ceiling extraction
- Double-counted partitions

**If fails:** severity = "critical" — Phase 2 extraction is unreliable.

---

### Rule 2: PAINT_AREA_REASONABLE

**Check:** dazomas_plotas_m2 / bendras_plotas_m2 should be in [1.5, 4.0]

**Why:** Paint area = (wall perimeter × height) + ceiling area - openings.
For typical rooms:
- Small apartment (ratio ~3.5): lots of walls relative to floor
- Medium house (ratio ~1.7): larger open spaces
- Complex house (ratio ~2.0-2.4): extra walls from niches, arches

**If fails:**
- Ratio <1.5: severely underestimated, critical error
- Ratio >4.0: likely double-counted or wrong units

---

### Rule 3: CORNERS_REASONABLE

**Check:** kampai_m / dazomas_plotas_m2 should be in [0.3, 1.5]

**Why:**
- Simple geometry: ~0.5 m corners per m² paint
- Average: ~0.7 m per m²
- Complex with arches/niches: up to 1.5 m per m²

**If fails:**
- <0.3: missed arches, curves, or niches
- >1.5: double-counted or wrong unit

---

### Rule 4: ANGOKRASCIAI_VS_OPENINGS

**Check:** angokrasciai_m / (doors_count + windows_count) should be in [4.0, 8.0]

**Why:** Each door/window opening has ~5-7 linear meters of angokraščiai (top + 2 sides = ~5m for standard 900mm door, more for wider openings).

**If fails:** Either angokraščiai over-extracted or doors/windows under-counted.

---

### Rule 5: TILES_IN_BATHROOMS

**Check:** plyteliu_plotas_m2 / (sum of bathroom floor areas) should be in [4.0, 8.0]

**Why:** Tiles cover both floors and walls of wet rooms. Typical coverage ~6x floor area.

**If fails:** Either missed wall tiles, or non-bathroom areas tiled.

---

### Rule 6: GKP_SHEETS_MATCH_AREA

**Check:** If ceiling GKP is specified, sheet count should be ceil(gkp_lubu_m2 × 1.10 / 3.0)

**Why:** GKP sheet = 1200×2500mm = 3 m². Waste factor 10%.

This is a deterministic calculation, should always pass if inputs correct.
