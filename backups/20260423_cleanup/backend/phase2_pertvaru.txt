CRITICAL RULES FOR CORNERS (most important for accuracy):

A "corner" in painting context = any place where two wall surfaces meet at an angle, requiring separate painting/protection.

**Types of corners:**

1. **INNER CORNERS (vidiniai, concave, <180°):**
   Walls going INTO a room. Like a normal room corner where you stand in the middle and look at the wall meeting point.

2. **OUTER CORNERS (išoriniai, convex, >180°):**
   Walls sticking OUT into a room. Usually at partitions between rooms, or around pillars/columns.

3. **ARCH/CURVE CORNERS:**
   An arch (R450, R600) creates 2 corner transitions: where the curve starts and where it ends.
   A full semicircle (R600 going from one wall to another) = 2 corners.
   A quarter arch = 2 corners.

4. **NICHE CORNERS:**
   Each wall niche creates 4 corners:
   - 2 outer corners where the niche starts (on the main wall)
   - 2 inner corners inside the niche (at the back corners)

---

HOW TO COUNT — step by step:

**Step 1:** Identify all rooms on this plan (use Phase 1 context).

**Step 2:** For EACH room, walk its perimeter mentally and count every angle change:

Example for a simple rectangular 4m × 5m room:
- 4 corners (all inner/concave 90°)
- visu_kampu_vnt for this room: 4
- isoriniu_kampu_vnt: 0 (all inner)

Example for room with alcove/niche:
- Main 4 corners
- +4 corners for niche (2 outer where it opens, 2 inner at back)
- visu_kampu_vnt: 8
- isoriniu_kampu_vnt: 2

Example for room with R450 arch entrance:
- 2 corners where normal walls are (excluding arch area)
- +2 corners where arch starts/ends
- Plus remaining normal corners

**Step 3:** Sum across ALL rooms.

WHY THIS MATTERS:

In the estimates, "dažymo kampų montavimas" is measured in LINEAR METERS (m), not count. Each corner is ~2.8m (from floor to ceiling). So:

- 70 corners × 2.8m = ~196m linear corners
- 210 corners × 2.8m = ~588m linear corners

For reference — REAL projects:
- P1 (Bacevičiaus): 580.54m corners (complex_house with arches)
- P2 (Čiurliu): 201.86m corners (medium_house)
- P5 (Lazūnų): 123.59m corners (small_apartment)

The calculation in the estimate = visu_kampu_vnt × room_height(usually 2.8m).

WINDOW DETECTION:

**For each window, extract:**
