You are analyzing Lithuanian interior construction drawings for quantity takeoff.

YOUR TASK: Find the ROOM TABLE (PATALPŲ EKSPLIKACIJA) in the drawings and extract:
1. All rooms with numbers, names (in Lithuanian), and floor areas in m²
2. Total floor area (bendras plotas) - with AND without garage if applicable
3. Ceiling heights (default 2.8m, but flag rooms with >3m ceilings)
4. Project complexity class
5. Special features that affect quantity calculations

---

CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR ROOM TABLE EXTRACTION:

The room table is almost always labeled "PATALPŲ EKSPLIKACIJA" (sometimes shortened to just a list with room numbers).

It typically contains columns:
  Nr. | Pavadinimas | Plotas
  01  | Tambūras    | 7,36 m²
  02  | Svetainė/valgomasis/virtuvė | 62,42 m²
  ...

**Important parsing rules:**
- Lithuanian decimal separator is comma (,) - convert to dot in your output
- "m²" suffix should be stripped
- Extract EVERY row - including garage, utility rooms, closets
- The table may appear on MULTIPLE pages - combine all unique rooms
- Some projects split table across drawings - look on partition plan, furniture plan, and sometimes ceiling plan
- If you see "Bendras namo plotas (su garažu) 224,00 m²" and "Bendras namo plotas (be garažo) 180,00 m²" - these are summary rows, use them for bendras_plotas_m2 fields

**Where to find the room table:**
- Partition plan (pertvarų/naujai statomų pertvarų planas) - MOST LIKELY
- Furniture layout (baldų išdėstymo planas) - SECOND MOST LIKELY
- Ceiling plan, electrical plan, other plans - SOMETIMES

---

COMPLEXITY CLASSIFICATION:

**small_apartment:** bendras_plotas < 60 m² AND patalpų <= 5
  Example: 41 m² studio with 4 rooms

**medium_house:** 60-250 m², no arches, no >3m ceilings, simple geometry
  Example: straight-walled 224 m² house with 15 rooms, standard ceiling height

**complex_house:** ANY of these features:
  - Arches or curves (look for radius notations like R450, R600, R150)
  - Ceiling niches for LED lighting (look for "MAZGAS A/B/C" labels or "niša LED")
  - Ceiling height > 3m anywhere (check "aukštis virš 3m" or altitude notations)
  - More than 3 bathrooms/WCs in the room list
  - Garage with high ceilings (>2.5m)
  - Skylights (stoglangiai)
  - Multiple staircase levels
  - Semi-circular or curved walls (R600 arc in living room, etc.)

**premium:** ANY of these keywords anywhere in specifications or material lists:
  - "Benjamin Moore" or "Supreme Ultimate" (premium paints)
  - "Stiklo audinys" (glass fiber cloth on ceilings)
  - "Moldingai" (crown moldings)
  - "Schuco/gola profilis" (premium kitchen profile)
  - "HPL compact" panels
  - "Paslėptos durys" (hidden/flush doors)
  - Premium tiles 1200×1200mm or larger with specific brand names

IMPORTANT: A project can be BOTH complex AND premium. In that case, return "premium" as it implies higher-end work.

---

FEATURE DETECTION TIPS:

Look at ALL drawing pages, not just the first one. Features appear on different pages:

- **Arches/curves:** Look at partition plans and wall elevations (išklotinės). Radius indicators like "R450", "R600" are visible.
- **LED niches:** Check ceiling plans and wall elevations. Look for "MAZGAS" labels with cross-section showing profile details.
- **High ceilings:** Check notes on partition plan. "aukštis virš 3m" or "lubų aukštis 3200mm"
- **Premium materials:** Check specifications tables, material legends ("BALDŲ MEDŽIAGIŠKUMAS")
