# LLM Council Transcript — Sąmatų AI Marketing Strategy

**Date:** 2026-05-13
**Topic:** Pressure-test 90-day bootstrap marketing strategy ("Hybrid Twin-Engine")

---

## The Question

Pressure-test 90-day bootstrap marketing strategy for Sąmatų AI — Lithuanian B2B SaaS, AI Vision reads PDF construction drawings → quantities. Solo founder, €0-200/month bootstrap budget, persona = 40-55yr LT contractor (currently uses incumbent Sistela). Pre-revenue beta with known accuracy bugs (ceiling >3m fails on p1).

**Strategy ("Hybrid Twin-Engine"):**
- Track 1 (SEO): Custom domain, prerender React landing, 8-10 trade-specific landing pages, 24-30 LT blog posts, magnet ebook, custom Claude Code SEO agent running nightly
- Track 2 (Community): Daily 10-15 min FB "Statybininkai", weekly demo posts, SA.lt/VŽ pitches, vev.lt Sistela trainer partnership, Skaitmeninė Statyba 2026 conf (~€300)
- Positioning: "Pirmasis lietuviškas AI sąmatininkas", "feeds Sistela not replaces", outcome-led, public EUR pricing (~€29-49/mo), self-serve trial
- Metrics (90d): 5 paying customers, 25-30 indexed pages, top-10 ranking on 3 LT keywords, 10 demo calls, 1 SA.lt or VŽ feature

**Specific questions:** (1) Is "feed Sistela not replace" positioning correct? (2) FB+SEO right engines, or should outbound replace one? (3) €1k/mo unlock? (4) Are 90-day metrics realistic? (5) Single biggest blind spot?

---

## Advisor Responses

### The Contrarian
The fatal flaw: you have no evidence anyone wants this enough to pay for it, and your strategy assumes they do for 90 days.

Five paying customers in 90 days from cold SEO + FB lurking, sold to a 45-year-old contractor who reads on his phone between site visits, doesn't trust the internet, and already has Sistela working "fine"? You're rationalizing a content marathon as customer discovery. It isn't.

Specific holes:
1. "Feeds Sistela" is cope. If you don't replace Sistela, you're a €29/mo add-on to a tool they already paid for and trained on. Add-ons die. The honest pitch is "skip the samatininko," and your real buyer isn't the 45-year-old phone-reader — it's the owner paying that samatininko €1500/mo. You've targeted the wrong persona with the wrong wedge.
2. "24-30 blog posts in LT for keywords nobody searches." Have you pulled actual search volume on "kiekių žiniaraštis programa"? My bet: <50/mo. You'll rank #1 on a desert. The "nightly Claude Code SEO agent" is founder-as-engineer escapism.
3. FB "answer one question daily" = 90 answers × ~2% conversion to a call = 1-2 demos. Not 10.
4. Beta still misses ceiling heights >3m on page 1. You're marketing a product that fails the first real test.

What I'd do instead: Kill Track 1 entirely for 30 days. Hand-deliver the tool to 10 contractors via warm intros. If 2 won't pay €49 after seeing it on their PDF, the marketing strategy is irrelevant — you don't have a product yet.

### The First Principles Thinker
You're not solving a marketing problem. You're solving a "do I have a business" problem and dressing it up as marketing because marketing feels like progress.

The real question is: does anyone pay money to have their PDF turned into quantities? You don't know yet. Pre-revenue. So everything in this plan that isn't "get a stranger to pay €29 this week" is procrastination wearing a strategy hat.

24-30 blog posts and an SEO agent running nightly is a 3-month bet that LT contractors search Google for "AI sąmatos" — a category that doesn't exist in their head yet. You're SEO-optimizing for demand that hasn't been created. That's backwards.

The segment definition is broken. "Solo samatininkai + 3-15 contractors + 15-50 firms" is three different products. A solo samatininkas is your competitor's user, not buyer — they get replaced, so they'll knife it in FB groups. The 15-50 firm has procurement and won't touch €29/mo. The 3-15 contractor-owner who currently pays a samatininkas €200-500/project is the only buyer who makes sense. Pick one.

"Feeds Sistela not replaces" — that's a cope. It's you flinching from the real pitch ("you can fire your samatininko or do 3× the bids").

Kill the metric "5 paying customers in 90 days." Replace with: 10 paid pilots in 30 days, sourced by you personally via DM, at full price, no discount.

### The Expansionist
This founder is sandbagging. "5 paying customers in 90 days" is the metric of someone optimizing for not-failing, not for catching a wave.

The real prize they're missing: APVA is a printing press. The renovation pipeline requires KŽ output. Don't "wedge" APVA — own it. Become the APVA-compliant KŽ generator, lobby APVA to mention you in their guidance docs. That's 50+ paying customers in 90 days, not 5.

The LT corpus is a generational moat. Every PDF processed teaches the model Lithuanian drafting conventions, Sistela-compatible position codes, LT-specific symbols. In 18 months no Western player can catch up without buying you. Togal raised at $35M doing this for English.

Baltic is not a stretch goal — it's Tuesday. Latvian/Estonian drafting conventions are 80% identical. Polish is the real prize: 10× the market.

The SEO agent is a product. Lithuanian B2B SaaS founders would pay €200/mo for a Claude Code agent that ships LT-language SEO. Sell it.

Stop thinking "5 customers." Think "default KŽ tool of the Baltic renovation wave."

### The Outsider
"Feeds Sistela not replaces Sistela" — I have no idea what this means. So your pitch is "buy a second tool that helps the first tool work better"? Right now "feeds" is jargon. Feeds it what? Bird food?

"Sąmatininko klaidų kaina: 14 pozicijų..." — industry-insider title. A 50-year-old contractor on a site doesn't download ebooks. He watches a 90-second video of someone dragging a PDF onto a screen and numbers appearing.

Homepage expectation: I'd expect to see a PDF on the left, a spreadsheet on the right, and an arrow. That's it. Not "Pirmasis lietuviškas AI sąmatininkas" — that's a press release headline.

Biggest blind spot: The buyer isn't on Facebook reading construction posts at lunch. He's the owner who hates that estimating eats his weekends. Reachable through his accountant or his industry association, not "Statybininkai" group banter.

Also: "AI sąmatos" as an SEO keyword — does anyone search that yet?

### The Executor

24 posts × 3.5h + 12 landing pages × 4h + daily FB + SEO agent build (80h) + ebook (20h) + prerender (15h) + self-serve trial (40h) + 10 demos (20h) + PR (15h) = ~340h of marketing/infra, before fixing the beta.

Cut list, in order:
1. SEO agent. Premature automation. Save 80h.
2. Ebook. No traffic to capture. Defer to Mo 4. Save 20h.
3. Conference sponsorship. €€ + zero attribution at this scale. Cut.
4. Cut blog cadence to 1/week.
5. Trade landing pages: 4, not 8-10.

Monday 9am literal first action: Google Keyword Planner LT terms, write top-volume post by Friday on $10 domain. SEO agent MVP (if Wk 4+): one cron, weekly Slack of SERP delta, 6h build.

FB daily is the only thing keeping you honest. Don't cut it.

## Chairman Verdict

### Where the Council Agrees
1. **Product isn't proven, marketing plan is premature.** Beta fails on real PDFs (ceiling >3m).
2. **"Feeds Sistela" is cope.** Three advisors used the word. Real wedge is "fire your samatininko" or "3× bid throughput."
3. **Persona is three buyers stacked.** Only the 3-15 contractor-OWNER (currently pays €200-500/project) is the actual buyer.
4. **24-30 posts + nightly SEO agent = founder-as-engineer escapism.** Search volume likely sub-100/mo.
5. **5 paying customers in 90 days is the wrong metric.**

### Where the Council Clashes
- **Sandbagging vs delusion:** Expansionist sees a generational moat; everyone else flags it as fantasy at current stage. Both are right about different time horizons.
- **Replace vs co-opt samatininkas:** Contrarian/First Principles say replace. Reviewer 4 says freelance samatininkai = distribution channel.
- **Track 1 (SEO) status:** Contrarian says kill entirely; Executor says cut 60%; First Principles says SEO captures category 12mo after FB creates it.

### Blind Spots the Council Caught
1. No evident sales pipeline — building the contact list IS the first deliverable.
2. Buyer ≠ User — owner is reachable via accountants, APVA GCs, LinkedIn; not FB Statybininkai.
3. Product validation milestone missing — what's the unattended success rate on stranger PDFs today?
4. Samatininkas as channel partner, not enemy.
5. Sistela integration claim unverified — "feeds Sistela" rests on an unrun test.

### The Recommendation
**Scrap the 90-day plan. Replace with a 30-day validation sprint, then re-plan.**

**Days 1-7 — Empirical reality check (no marketing):** Run beta unattended on 10 real contractor PDFs. Measure success rate. <70% kills the marketing plan. Run Sistela round-trip test. Pull actual Google Keyword Planner volumes.

**Days 8-30 — Hand-sell to 10 owners (not users):** 3-15 employee contractor-owners. Channels: warm intros + LinkedIn + accountants + APVA GCs. Pitch: "Skip the samatininko" or "Bid 3× more tenders." Goal: 3 owners pay €49 after seeing tool on their own PDF.

**Days 31-90 — Re-plan with data.** If 30-day validation hits: 1 weekly blog post, FB for learning not sales, samatininkas-reseller experiment, one APVA-adjacent partnership.

**Kill list:** SEO agent (-80h), ebook (-20h), conference (-€300), 8-10 trade landing pages → 0 until Mo 2, 24-30 blog posts → 0 in Mo 1 then 1/week, "feeds Sistela" positioning, "5 paying customers" as 90-day metric.

### The One Thing to Do First
**Monday 9am: Run the current beta, unattended, on 5 real contractor PDFs you have never seen before. Score success/failure honestly. Write the numbers down. Email them to yourself.**

If it scores <70%, close the marketing tab. The next 30 days are product, not promotion.
