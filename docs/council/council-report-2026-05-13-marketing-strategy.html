Sąmatų AI Marketing Strategy — Council Verdict

2026-05-13 · 5 advisors · 5 peer reviews · chairman synthesis

The Question

Pressure-test 90-day bootstrap marketing strategy "Hybrid Twin-Engine" (SEO content + nightly Claude Code SEO agent + daily FB "Statybininkai" + SA.lt PR + €300 conf). Positioning: "Pirmasis lietuviškas AI sąmatininkas", "feeds Sistela not replaces". Metrics: 5 paying customers, 25-30 indexed pages, top-10 on 3 LT keywords. Solo founder, €0-200/mo, pre-revenue beta with known accuracy bugs.

Verdict

Where the Council Agrees

  • Product isn't proven, marketing plan is premature. Beta fails ceiling >3m on p1.
  • "Feeds Sistela" is cope. Real wedge: "fire your samatininko" or "3× bid throughput".
  • Persona is three buyers stacked. Only 3-15 contractor-OWNER (pays €200-500/project) is the buyer.
  • 24-30 posts + nightly SEO agent = engineer escapism. Volume likely sub-100/mo.
  • "5 paying customers in 90 days" is the wrong metric.

Where the Council Clashes

Sandbagging vs delusion. Expansionist: APVA + Baltic + corpus moat. Everyone else: fantasy at current stage. Both right about different time horizons.

Replace vs co-opt the samatininkas. Replace says Contrarian/First Principles. Co-opt as reseller says peer reviewer 4.

Track 1 SEO status. Kill entirely (Contrarian), cut 60% (Executor), or run 12-month lag indicator (First Principles).

Blind Spots Caught in Peer Review

  • No evident sales pipeline — building the contact list is the first deliverable.
  • Buyer ≠ User. Owner reachable via accountants, APVA GCs, LinkedIn — not FB.
  • Product validation milestone missing entirely.
  • Samatininkas as channel partner, not just enemy.
  • Sistela integration claim is unverified.

The Recommendation

Scrap the 90-day plan. Replace with 30-day validation sprint.

  • Days 1-7: Run beta unattended on 10 unseen PDFs. Sistela round-trip test. Pull real keyword volumes.
  • Days 8-30: Hand-sell to 10 owners (3-15 emp). Channels: warm intros + LinkedIn + accountants + APVA GCs. Pitch: "Skip the samatininko". Goal: 3 owners pay €49.
  • Days 31-90: Re-plan with data. 1 weekly blog post, FB for learning not sales, samatininkas-reseller experiment, APVA partnership convo.

Kill list: SEO agent (-80h), ebook (-20h), conference (-€300), 8-10 LPs → 0 until Mo 2, 24-30 posts → 0 in Mo 1, "feeds Sistela", "5 paying customers" metric.

Monday 9am · The one thing: Run current beta, unattended, on 5 real contractor PDFs you have never seen. Score success/failure honestly. Email yourself the numbers. If <70%, close the marketing tab — next 30 days are product, not promotion.
