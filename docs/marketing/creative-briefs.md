# Pathon — video + photo briefs (2026-07-06)

All spoken/on-screen text is DRAFT LT — founder polishes before recording.
Gear: screen recording (OBS/QuickTime) + phone camera is enough. Brand: graphite
bg, orange accents, JetBrains-mono numbers — match the app, not stock-SaaS look.

## Video briefs (10)

**V1 · Hero demo — "PDF → sąmata" (45–60 s, YouTube + Landing + ads)**
Screencap, no face, LT captions, no music-only-hype.
Beats: (1) real PDF drops in, 2 s; (2) timelapse of extraction progress 8 s;
(3) rooms appear with colored zones 8 s; (4) confidence flags close-up — "žalia
— nuskaityta, geltona — patikrink" 8 s; (5) price column typed in 5 s;
(6) Excel export opens with live formulas 8 s; (7) end-card: pitch + "pirmas
projektas nemokamas" + pathon.eu 5 s. Hook (first 2 s, text): **"Sąmata iš
brėžinio per 15 minučių. Rodau."**

**V2 · Flood-fill satisfying (15 s, TikTok/Reels/Shorts)**
Just the zone-painting animation on a real plan, sped up, trending audio,
caption: "Kai kiekiai susiskaičiuoja patys 🧱". End frame: logo + "pathon.eu".
Zero selling. This is the repeatable format — every new project = new clip.

**V3 · Split-screen "3 dienos vs 15 minučių" (30 s, FB/LinkedIn)**
Left: paberti brėžiniai, skaičiuotuvas, Excel ranka (staged, 6 clips). Right:
upload → done. Ending: "Tas pats žiniaraštis. €300 vs €12."

**V4 · Founder story (60–90 s, LinkedIn/YouTube, face to camera)**
Beats: kodėl (mačiau, kiek laiko/€ statybininkai degina sąmatoms) → kas sunkiausia
(tikslumas; kaip sprendžiam su pažymėjimais, ne pažadais) → kvietimas (10 pilotų).
Honest > polished; one take is fine.

**V5 · Tutorial series (5 × 3–5 min, YouTube — the SEO library)**
1. Įkėlimas ir pirmas projektas · 2. Prielaidos ir perskaičiavimas ·
3. Kiekių taisymas ir pažymėjimai · 4. Kainos + eksportas (XLSX/PDF) ·
5. Matavimai žiūryklėje + kalibravimas. Each titled as a search query.

**V6 · "AI vs rangovas" accuracy challenge (2–3 min, the trust asset)**
A real contractor brings a finished project with THEIR sąmata; run the PDF live;
compare line by line on camera; show hits AND misses honestly (the ~1% formula
story + detection flags). This video does more for trust than any ad. Needs one
willing pilot — offer free year.

**V7 · Feature shorts (3 × 20 s):** durys/angokraščiai read from tags; plytelių
zonos iš išklotinių; LED nišos iš lubų plano. Format: zoom into drawing → number
appears → one-line caption.

**V8 · "Ar galima pasitikėti AI kiekiais?" (60 s, objection killer)**
Walk the confidence system: žalia/geltona/raudona, "Tikrinti pirmiausia" list,
one-click fix, recompute. Ending: "Nesakome 'pasitikėk'. Sakome 'patikrink per
5 minutes'."

**V9 · "5 sąmatos klaidos" listicle video (45 s, FB groups gold)**
From CLAUDE.md §8: pamiršta hidroizoliacija, gruntas atskirai, Q4 šlifavimas,
angokraščiai, atliekų %. Each = 1 screen + 1 line. CTA-free (value post).

**V10 · Pilot testimonial template (30–45 s)**
Questions to ask on camera: kiek laiko užtrukdavo anksčiau? kas nustebino?
kam rekomenduotum? one number they saved. Film vertical + horizontal.
