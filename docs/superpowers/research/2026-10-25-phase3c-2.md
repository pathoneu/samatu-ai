## 5. Follow-ups (not built — measured reasons)

1. **Legend glyph fragments** (d pendant 16 vs 2: the row's glyph box holds a piece of the ⊗) and **text-glyph
   counts** („T" thermostats 18 on a) are the remaining over-counts; a glyph-completeness check in `legend_rows`
   (the row's glyph must be the whole cluster in the band) is the next €0 step.
2. **Wall finishes need geometry, not text** (a's paint, §0.2): the finish plan draws each finish as a coloured
   line along the walls and names the colour in its legend (`legend_rows` already reads the rows and colours); the
   length of each colour inside a room's outline (the lane's outline, sheet alignment exists) is the share. That is
   the R2-legal replacement of the image estimate.
3. **Plumbing without a legend** (every corpus plumbing plan): the fixtures are drawn as furniture blocks with prose
   labels („Praustuvas, maišytuvas iš praustuvo"). Counting them deterministically means block matching against the
   sanitary-ware library or a label-to-position parser; until then the rows are blank with the room-type count in
   the sentence.
4. **Pass-1** (§0.1) — the rooms, openings and glances still come from the model; ending it is the plan's last
   Phase 3 step.
