# Togal.AI vs Kreo 2D Takeoff — user-experience walkthrough (as of 2026-09-17)

## Executive summary

1. Both are "AI first, then correct" polygon editors: one button (Togal) or a per-category/prompt run (Kreo) drops coloured polygons, lines and count dots on the sheet plus a quantities list; the user then reclassifies and drags vertices.
2. Togal: green button → "10-15 seconds" → Footprint / Net Area / Gross Area (wall centreline) / Doors Area, wall-centreline + wall-perimeter + door-centreline lines, and counts (plumbing fixtures, doors, furniture, appliances) — [help](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15602961-the-togal-button-togal-s-automated-takeoff). Architectural floor plans only ([Eos](https://eosgroup.com/etakeoff-dimension-with-togal-ai/)).
3. Kreo: Auto Measure runs per category (rooms, doors/windows, wall finishes, walls, GIA/NIA, custom text prompt, objects), shows the AI "working" on the sheet with seconds remaining ([AM 2.0](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/9722231-auto-measure-20)); AM 3.0 (28 Aug 2026) publishes outline accuracy 72→94 %, area captured 92.6→99.3 % ([blog](https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/auto-measure-3-0-best-ai-model-quantity-takeoff)).
4. Scale: both auto-suggest and require a human confirm; Togal states the OCR "does NOT test the correctness of the scale" ([help](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15367669-setting-up-the-scale)); Kreo recalculates every measurement when scale changes ([help](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5487476-scale-for-drawings)).
5. Room names: Kreo names polygons from PDF text (vector only, else "Undefined"); Togal suggests a classification from ML, no documented label-reading.
6. Confidence: Kreo has an AI-confidence slider (AM 2.0) and similarity % per Auto Count match; Togal shows no per-item confidence (not found).
7. Corrections: both have vertex drag/add/delete, split/merge/subtract, right-click reassign, undo. Neither shows a "what changed downstream" diff; Togal's version history has no change log.
8. Perimeter: Kreo polygon = area + perimeter; vertical area = perimeter × height with no documented opening deduction. Togal emits separate Wall Perimeter / Wall Centreline lines; doors are backed out through classification dimensions/formulas.
9. Schedules: Kreo extracts tables to a spreadsheet report and cross-references tags to legends but does not reconcile with geometry; Togal.CHAT answers from specs but "cannot perform takeoff".
10. Pricing: Togal $299/user/mo billed yearly; Kreo $35/$70/$175 per user/mo billed annually, AI only on Pro. Both draw the same complaint: raster/scanned plans and "messy" output that must be re-organised.

## Togal.AI

**1. Upload.** PDF recommended; raster PDFs "may lose clarity when zoomed in, and snapping features will not be available"; PNG/JPEG/TIFF single-page; DWG only via PDF export ([file types](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15396922-supported-file-types)). On upload Togal is "running its AI to detect the drawing number and auto naming from the title block" ([upload](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15313293-uploading-drawings)); naming can be undone/edited ([auto naming](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15438222-automated-drawing-naming)). Page-type classification (plan/elevation/schedule): **not found**. Scale: "Togal's OCR technology ... will automatically locate the scale written in the drawings", but "the system does NOT test the correctness"; verify with the Measure tool; manual = draw a known line ("Snap to standard scales") or type the ratio; "You can't manually apply one scale to the whole set" ([scale](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15367669-setting-up-the-scale)).

**2. AI detection.** "Ensure the drawing scale is set correctly", then the green button "runs AI takeoff and places the measurement results onto your drawing and into the Quantities Panel"; "Processing typically takes 10-15 seconds" ([Togal button](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15602961-the-togal-button-togal-s-automated-takeoff)). Outputs: "Net Area: The interior finish area of each room", "Gross Area ... measured from wall centerline to wall centerline", Footprint, Doors Area; lines "Wall Centerline", "Wall Perimeter: A linear takeoff tracing along wall faces/edges", "Doors Centerline" ([area & wall detection](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15602901-ai-powered-area-and-wall-detection)). Walls auto-classify into "Exterior, Shaft, Corridor, Interior, Demising, Bathroom"; "works best when the plan is clearly labeled and wall geometry is clean" ([wall centreline](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15611774-autoclassify-wall-centerline)). Counts: symbol/pattern search — "draw a bounding box around the object, then 'search' your entire plan set" ([features](https://www.togal.ai/how-it-works)). "Works only for architectural floor plans. It does not work for ... electrical, landscape, structural" ([Eos](https://eosgroup.com/etakeoff-dimension-with-togal-ai/)). Claims: "up to 98% accuracy", "5x faster" ([togal.ai](https://www.togal.ai/)); University of Kansas blind study: 76 % faster than On-Screen Takeoff, "Percentage difference within 5%", but "Common AI inconsistencies include missed projections, confusing annotations, and the inclusion of unrelated elements" ([case study](https://www.togal.ai/case-study/ku-study-togal-vs-ost)). Vector vs raster accuracy split: **not published**.

**3. Presentation.** Polygons/lines carry classification colour, "fill pattern, line style" ([editing](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15463813-editing-and-modifying-takeoffs)). Quantities panel (top right) lists classifications with "the total quantity for that item across the filtered range"; expand to instances; "Clicking on a specific takeoff instance in the panel will select and highlight it on the drawing" and vice versa ([panel](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15611909-quantities-panel-overview)); eye icon show/hide, Ctrl+click eye to isolate ([panel](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15601691-togal-s-quantities-panel)). Per-room table with area+perimeter side by side: only via up to "8 output quantities" per classification (Area/Perimeter/Volume units) ([properties](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15504397-creating-and-editing-classification-properties)). Confidence indicator: **not found**.

**4. Verification & correction.** Drag vertex; "Hover your cursor over a line segment ... A new point will appear"; "Double click on a vertex to remove it"; counts: drag, Delete, re-click to add; right-click to reassign ([editing](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15463813-editing-and-modifying-takeoffs)). Split S, Merge M, Cut/Subtract C ([blog](https://www.togal.ai/blog/how-to-improve-the-efficiency-of-your-precon-workflow-using-the-editing-tools-in-togal-ai)); deduction shows as "solid white space", net value updates in the panel ([deductions](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15461267-deducting-an-area-or-length-from-a-takeoff)). Auto Classify: right-click → suggestion (e.g. "Lobby Elevator"); "If the AI was not able to classify ... it will not change" ([blog](https://www.togal.ai/blog/how-to-auto-classify-areas-using-the-automatic-classification-feature-of-togal-ai)). Shortcuts: A/L/C tools, Ctrl+Z, Shift ortho, Enter finish ([shortcuts](https://help.togal.ai/shortcuts)). Re-Togaling re-runs AI; "If a Togal classification was re-assigned, your changes will be saved", the original "will be re-generated, resulting in a duplicate" ([re-togaling](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15603137-re-togaling)). Version history restores polygons but "cannot recover deleted classifications"; no diff/change log ([versions](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15453610-version-history-and-snapshot)). Downstream "what changed" indicator: **not found**.

**5. Schedules.** Togal.CHAT answers "using information found in your uploaded drawings and specs" but "cannot perform takeoff" ([chat](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15603179-togal-chat)). Drywall assemblies "ingest drawing wall schedules to identify wall tags" ([Rathmann, Sep 2026](https://www.rathmanninsights.com/post/what-s-new-and-cool-with-togal-ai-takeoff-ai-for-drywall)). Room-schedule vs polygon reconciliation: **not found**.

**6. Perimeter/walls.** Three AI line types (centreline, perimeter along faces, door centreline) — see §2. Openings: door dims per count classification ("Height as 7 ft, Length as 3 ft") or "door height x door centerline length" formula ([backout](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15611664-how-to-backout-doors-and-windows)); or split a wall line and delete the segment ([deductions](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15461267-deducting-an-area-or-length-from-a-takeoff)). Fixing: "delete any incorrect lines, connect any small gaps, and reclassify" ([detection](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15602901-ai-powered-area-and-wall-detection)).

**7. Export & pricing.** Excel with "up to 4 groupings", filters, "Screen View" vs "Full Export" ([Excel](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15611424-exporting-takeoff-data-to-excel)); PDF with overlays and legend ([PDF](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15611454-export-annotated-drawings-to-pdf)); integrations ServiceTitan, Zebel, Ediphi ([getting started](https://help.togal.ai/en/collections/19647627-getting-started)), eTakeoff Dimension ([eTakeoff](https://support.etakeoff.com/kb/article/416-07-togal-ai-length-measurement/)). Growth "$299 / mo per user, billed yearly"; Business custom "for teams with 4+ users" ([pricing](https://www.togal.ai/pricing)); no free trial ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/togal-ai/pricing)).

**8. Reported limits.** G2: "automatic take-offs sometimes capture more items or items that were not intended to be captured, causing rework"; not ready for civil/sitework ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/togal-ai/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons)). Aggregated-review article: "about 85% accurate on the residential floors and about 60% ... on the retail podium"; "still figuring out when to trust it"; scanned plans "manual correction takes longer than starting from scratch" ([Struvia](https://struvia.co/blog/togal-ai-review-2026), not hands-on). Trustpilot single 1-star: "Simple Takeoff Tool Masquerading as Advanced AI" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.togal.ai)). eTakeoff: "classifications might not correspond with actual components ... use them as a starting point" ([kb](https://support.etakeoff.com/kb/article/415-06-togal-ai-net-area-measurement/)).

## Kreo 2D Takeoff

**1. Upload.** "PDF, DWG, DXF, DWF, DGN, PNG, TIFF, JPG, JPEG, BMP, EMF, GIF"; "reads native PDF and CAD vector data (not rasterized images)" ([what is Kreo](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5723499-what-is-kreo-software)). Zone Mapping "detects and separates drawing zones — main drawing area, legends, tables, title blocks, and notes" ([kreo.net](https://www.kreo.net/)). Rename with AI reads title blocks; "If there is no text or the text is embedded as geometry, the AI will not be able to read it" ([rename](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/10371463-rename-with-ai)). Scale: AI "automatically suggests a scale ... once it's uploaded", user presses Apply; calibrate by known line; multi-page apply; "Any time you are changing the scale, all your measurements will be automatically recalculated" ([scale](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5487476-scale-for-drawings)).

**2. AI detection.** AM 1.0: "compatible only with architectural floor plans, especially residential", "30-60 seconds" ([AM](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5481199-auto-measure)); legacy add-on "Applicable for vector graphics only" ([add-on](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5835332-auto-measure-add-on)). AM 2.0 categories: Rooms & Areas (Include Doorways, Rename with AI), Doors & Windows ("Window 600mm, Door 1500mm", count or centreline), Wall Internal/External Finishes (polylines, Remove Doorways/Windows), GIA/GEA, NIA, Walls 2.0 (centreline; Remove Doors "Full Height" or "Door Height"; classify by thickness or hatch), Custom prompt; progress shown "where the AI is 'working' ... and how many seconds are left" ([AM 2.0](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/9722231-auto-measure-20)). AM 3.0 adds "a second pass" that "re-fits every outline to the lines of the drawing" and an Objects type ("type what to look for"); metrics vs 2.0: outline accuracy 72→94 %, area captured 92.6→99.3 %, usable results 81→90 %, coverage 81→87 %, narrow spans 0.42→0.67, measured "against labelled ground truth"; "switched on by request" ([AM 3.0](https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/auto-measure-3-0-best-ai-model-quantity-takeoff)). One-Click Area: click inside, positive/negative points, red bound, requires "AI Turbo Boost" ([OCA](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/8718150-one-click-area)). Auto Count: matches "with the similarity percentage displayed", slider, "Split by Text" ([Auto Count](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5481213-auto-count)). Caddie agent (May 2026): natural-language plan you approve, "pause, adjust, or safely undo the last action" ([Caddie](https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/caddie-the-ai-agent-that-does-the-work-instead-of-just-talking-about-it)).

**3. Presentation.** Polygons coloured with "same color indicates the same size"; names "based on the text provided in your PDF", else "Undefined" ([AM 2.0](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/9722231-auto-measure-20)); Measurements Manager groups rooms by type, doors/windows by width; "Auto Focus" jumps to the shape when selected ([settings](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5487492-drawing-settings)); "Show Dimensions" on shapes; on-sheet Drawing Legend of "Area, Perimeter, Length, Vertical Area, Count" ([legend](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/11688689-drawing-legend)); Grid/Spreadsheet reports "with a live connection" ([what is Kreo](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5723499-what-is-kreo-software)). Confidence: "Adjust AI confidence by simply dragging the slider" pre-create; similarity % on counts. Post-create per-item confidence: **not found**.

**4. Verification & correction.** Pre-create: slider, "Fix with AI", bin icon, Create ([AM 2.0](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/9722231-auto-measure-20)). Post-create: right-click → Modify; drag points; "click on it in the necessary place and an extra point will be immediately added"; Delete; Ctrl multi-select; align ([modify](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5481228-modify-measurements)). Merge Ctrl+Alt+U, Subtract Ctrl+Alt+S with "Keep polygons" ([subtract](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5481240-subtract-polygons)), Knife ([knife](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/6124741-knife-for-lines-and-polygons)), count items move/delete ([counts](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5756033-operations-with-count-items)), full shortcut list ([shortcuts](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5723771-keyboard-shortcuts)). Undo/redo "up to 25 actions during the current session", reset on refresh ([undo](https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/auto-move-to-cell-undo-redo)). Downstream change indicator: reports are live-linked, but no diff view (**not found**).

**5. Schedules.** "Extracting Schedules and Tables": click a table → AI Knowledge → "Export to Sheets" → spreadsheet report ([schedules](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/11688697-extracting-schedules-and-tables)); Cross Reference links tags to legend definitions and can "Count Matching Tags" ([cross ref](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/11688696-cross-reference)). Reconciliation of schedule areas vs polygons: **not found**.

**6. Perimeter/walls.** Polygon yields "area and perimeter" ([polygon](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5481180-polygon)); "Vertical Area = Perimeter × Height", height set per measurement/group, opening deduction not documented ([3D params](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/9611746-3d-parameters-for-measurements)). Wall finishes are polylines with Remove Doorways/Windows; walls are centreline with thickness (§2). Fixes: Extract Polyline from polygon, Split polyline into segments ([split](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/9595874-split-polyline-into-segments)), One-Click Line (beta, "sensitive to the exact point where you click") ([OCL](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/9993983-one-click-line)), API returns "perimeter" and wall "length, and thickness" ([API](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/11688711-using-the-ai-search-api-detecting-walls-doors-windows-and-rooms)).

**7. Export & pricing.** Excel ("formulae ... in usual Excel format", "links ... displayed as numbers") ([Excel](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5480818-exporting-the-schedule-to-excel)); PDF with layers, "Only visible", segment lengths ([PDF](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/5480823-exporting-drawings-into-pdf-format)); API on Pro "Talk with Sales"; Contractor Foreman. Lite $35 / Plus $70 / Pro $175 "per user per month billed annually"; Auto Measure, One-Click Area, Auto Count only on Pro; 7-day trial; free guest accounts ([pricing](https://www.kreo.net/pricing)). Target: "Quantity surveyors, cost estimators, and contractors".

**8. Reported limits.** Capterra 4.4/5 (25): "auto measure can be a little messy and i spend as much time organizing the data"; "The AI function is very hit and miss on the particular drawings we use"; "works best with vector drawings" vs scanned condo plans; "I could not figure out how to do a negative measure" ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/231342/Kreo-2D-Takeoff/reviews/)); "each time you start and stop, a new condition is created" ([Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/reviews/1014073/kreo-2d-takeoff)). G2 4.5/5 (32) ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/kreo/reviews)). Kreo's own blog warns against trusting "98% accuracy ... because a brochure said so" ([blog](https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/what-is-ai-takeoff)).

## Versus a per-room check sheet (six facts + crop + accept/correct)

**What Togal/Kreo do that a check sheet cannot**
- Geometry editing in place: vertex drag, split/merge/subtract, knife, negative points — a check sheet can only accept or type a number; it cannot fix *why* the area is wrong.
- Whole-sheet scope: footprint, GIA/NIA, corridors, wall centrelines with thickness, symbol counts across pages, drawing comparison — none is "per room".
- Re-runnable AI with an explicit prompt (Kreo custom/Objects, Caddie) and confidence pre-filtering before anything is created.
- Live downstream links: Kreo spreadsheet reports and Togal custom formulas recompute from the polygon; the check sheet's accept/correct must own that recompute itself.
- Multi-user, versioned, exportable evidence (annotated PDFs with legends).

**What a check sheet does that they cannot (per the sources)**
- Puts *height, finishes, doors/windows and floor* next to area/perimeter per room; both tools hold height as a property you type per group ([Kreo](https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/9611746-3d-parameters-for-measurements)) or a classification dimension ([Togal](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15611664-how-to-backout-doors-and-windows)), never as a reviewed fact.
- Per-fact accept/reject audit trail: neither shows post-create confidence, a change log, or what a correction changed downstream (all **not found**).
- Reconciles drawn tables (room schedule, finish schedule) against measured polygons; Kreo only exports the table, Togal only chats about it.
- Works identically on raster scans (crop + human eye) where both vendors and their reviewers concede AI degrades.
- Zero learning curve: G2/Capterra complaints centre on panels, "messy" output, and "still figuring out when to trust it"; a check sheet has one decision per fact.

**Honest caveat:** a check sheet cannot beat manual counting on correction speed for *geometry* errors; the KU study's "most adjustments ... in <5 min" ([KU](https://www.togal.ai/case-study/ku-study-togal-vs-ost)) is the bar to match, and it was set with a polygon editor, not accept/correct buttons.
