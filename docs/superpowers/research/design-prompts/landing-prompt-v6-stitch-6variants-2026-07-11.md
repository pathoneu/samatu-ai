Design the marketing landing page for **Pathon**. You get NO prescribed art

from the product, not from a template. Read all of it before designing.

## The product — every real detail (this is your design material)

Pathon is a Lithuanian SaaS for construction contractors. What it actually
does, end to end:

1. **Upload:** the user drops in a construction project PDF — interior design
   documentation, A1/A3 sheets, vector or scanned. Floor plans, wall
   developments (sienų išklotinės), ceiling plans, furniture plans, room
   schedules, specifications.
2. **Reading (~10–15 min):** AI classifies every page by type, finds the
   rooms, and reads the room schedule (patalpų eksplikacija). Detected rooms
   appear as translucent colored zones drawn directly ON the drawing —
   clickable, each linked to its numbers.
3. **Quantities:** it computes work quantities with the same formulas a
   professional Lithuanian estimator uses: paintable wall area with opening
   deductions, skim-coat by Q-level, drywall sheet counts, flooring with
   waste %, tile zones, baseboards, ceiling perimeters, window/door reveals
   (angokraščiai), LED cove lengths, waterproofing in wet rooms, electrical
   points. Results form a bill of quantities (kiekių žiniaraštis) — e.g. 46
   priced lines across 11 rooms.
4. **Trust system (the differentiator):** every line carries a confidence
   dot — green: read directly from the drawing; amber: computed with an
   assumption; red: a default was used. A "Tikrinti pirmiausia" queue
   surfaces exactly which few lines to review — "check 3 lines out of 46" —
   so the user verifies in minutes instead of trusting blindly.
5. **Assumptions, live:** a Prielaidos panel lists what was assumed (ceiling
   heights per room, finish quality level, window/door counts). Change any
   value and every affected quantity recalculates instantly on screen.
6. **The viewer is a real takeoff tool:** PDF plan viewer with page
   thumbnails, search, zoom; measurement tools (distance, area, perimeter,
   count) drawn on the sheet; scale calibration against a known dimension;
   room zones toggleable per layer.
7. **Pricing the work:** the user enters their own unit prices inline, or
   applies a saved price list (kainynas). Totals build live: per section, be
   PVM / su PVM (21%), with reverse-charge VAT for renovation projects.
8. **Output:** Excel export with LIVE formulas (change a quantity in Excel,
   sums recalculate) and a Lithuanian-format estimate PDF with section
   subtotals, VAT line, parties and signature blocks. Read-only share links
   for sending to a client or partner. An AI chat answers questions about
   the loaded project ("kiek dažų reikia 102 patalpai?").
9. **Pricing:** first project free for a company; €12 one-off per project;
   Pro €49/mo (10 projects); Studio €149/mo (35 projects). Prices be PVM.

Audience: Lithuanian construction company owners and contractors who do
their own takeoffs, evenings, after site work. The pitch that is true: what
takes them from several hours to a couple of days by hand happens in
minutes plus a short review.

Product mockups on the page must depict the REAL surfaces described above —
a drawing with colored room zones beside a quantities table with confidence
dots, the assumptions panel, the export — never an invented generic
dashboard. Demo data invented-neutral: "101 Tambūras 11,0 m²", "102
Svetainė/virtuvė 50,5 m²". No real client data. No testimonials, no invented
accuracy numbers, no user counts.

## Copy

Fresh, natural Lithuanian (draft ok — founder polishes; zero lorem, zero
English). Laws: the value is TIME — the user does this work themselves, so
never imply they pay someone else for estimates; Pathon is the tool, never
an add-on to another tool; never promise perfection — the checking system
IS the honesty ("patikrink 3 eilutes" beats "pasitikėk"). Voice: precise,
concrete, numbers over adjectives, zero hype words (jokių "atraskite",
"revoliucija", "seamless", "pakelkite į kitą lygį").

## The task: SIX different landing pages

Produce six full landing-page concepts. Each one must declare, before the
design: a concept name, its palette (your choice of hexes), its type pairing,
its signature animated moment, and its layout system. Then the complete page:
hero → product story sections → pricing → CTA → footer, desktop and mobile.

Hard rules that apply to ALL six:

- **Bright grounds only.** Every variant sits on a light/bright background —
  bright does not mean white-only: pale colors, warm papers, tinted grounds
  are all welcome. No dark-mode variants.
- **Maximally different from each other.** Six different palette families,
  six different hero structures, six different animation languages, six
  different grid systems. If two variants could be confused at a glance,
  replace one. At least: one variant where a huge animated product/drawing
  visual IS the hero; one typography-led variant where scale and type do the
  work; one with an unusual grid (diagonal, modular, or magazine-style); the
  other three are fully yours.
- **Eye-catching, not words-on-a-screen.** Every section pairs its message
  with something visual — a product frame, an animated diagram of the
  drawing-becoming-numbers, a live counter, a plan detail. If a section is
  only a headline and a paragraph, redesign it. The hero of every variant
  must contain a moment that makes a visitor stop scrolling.
- **Motion in every variant:** an animated hero sequence plus scroll-triggered
  reveals plus micro-interactions — each variant with its OWN motion
  personality (one might plot lines like a plotter, one might count numbers
  up, one might slide sheets like a flatbed scanner — invent them). Animate
  only transform/opacity; everything degrades gracefully under
  prefers-reduced-motion into a composed static page.
- Exactly one primary CTA per hero ("Išbandyti nemokamai"); nav has the
  Pathon wordmark + monogram-P (existing asset — recolor, don't redesign),
  "Prisijungti", and the CTA.
- Responsive: single column under 768px, no horizontal scroll, headline
  clamp() scaling, ≥44px touch targets.
- Numbers in a monospaced face with tabular figures — this is a product about
  numbers.

Banned in all six (instant rejection): Inter/Roboto/system fonts as display;
generic serifs (Times/Georgia/Garamond); purple-blue gradients; neon glows;
pure black (#000000); emojis; centered hero + three-equal-cards template;
"Scroll to explore" cues or bouncing chevrons; stock photos of builders
pointing at tablets; robots/brains/sparkles imagery; testimonial cards;
warm-cream + terracotta with a serif display (that is the current site — all
six must be different worlds from it and from each other); AI copy clichés;
fake round numbers; overlapping text on images.

## Deliverable

For each of the six: (1) concept name + 3-line rationale (palette-why,
type-why, motion-why), (2) the full desktop landing page, every section
designed with real Lithuanian copy and true product data, (3) the mobile
version. Six pages that could each be screenshotted and shared on their own
merits. The safe version of this page already exists — we built it. Show us
six pages we couldn't have imagined.
