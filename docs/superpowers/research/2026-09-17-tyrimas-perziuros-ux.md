# HITL review UIs: what makes checking AI-extracted data faster than doing it by hand (2026-09-17)

## Executive summary

- Review beats manual work only when (a) the human verifies against the **source**, not against the AI — "explanations are only useful to the extent that they allow a human decision maker to verify the correctness of an AI's prediction" ([Fok & Weld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07722)); (b) the human sees **exceptions**, not everything — Rossum ships a 0.975 default threshold and warns it yields "very little, if any, automation", and that confidence calibration is "still an unsolved problem", so thresholds must be tuned on your own data ([Rossum](https://knowledge-base.rossum.ai/docs/using-ai-confidence-thresholds-for-automation-in-rossum)); (c) accept/correct costs **one keystroke and shows its consequences**.
- Measured wins: source-anchored citations cut verification time ~20% ([SymGen, MIT](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2024/10/241022133244.htm)); interactive reasoning views raised error detection 73.5%→85.6% and cut time 64.7→57.9 s, N=125 ([arXiv 2510.22922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22922)); pre-annotation saved 13.9–21.5% per entity with no measurable bias ([Lingren 2014](https://academic.oup.com/jamia/article/21/3/406/2909253)) and 20–35% vs 11–21% ([Schulz 2019](https://aclanthology.org/P19-1265.pdf)) — but **zero** savings when the interface was wrong ([South 2014](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1532046414001191)).
- Stake-ordered queues pay off only under load (−32% turnaround in work hours, ~0 off-hours, 11,252 exams — [JACR 2025](https://www.jacr.org/article/S1546-1440(25)00481-8/abstract)) and starve the tail (max wait 890→1,178 min, normals 80→114 min — [Sci Rep sim](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8128725/)). Order by € at risk, but with a floor.
- For a per-room takeoff: fact cards with a drawing crop + one LT sentence; Enter = accept & next; a correction shows the downstream m²/kg/€ diff instantly; blanks-with-reason instead of low-confidence guesses; a per-fact-type column sweep; random re-check of auto-accepted rows. Measure seconds per fact, fixed-vs-accepted per fact type, € caught per minute, undo count.

## 1. Twelve patterns that measurably help

1. **Source snippet next to the value.** Verification cost, not explanation quality, is the binding constraint ([Fok & Weld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07722)); SymGen's cell-level citations made validation ~20% faster in a user study ([MIT](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2024/10/241022133244.htm)). Products: Azure returns a `source` bounding box per field so reviewers "jump directly to the source" ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/content-understanding/document/enrichments)); ABBYY shows the field's page image with zoom (Ctrl+Alt+I) ([ABBYY](https://help.abbyy.com/en-us/flexicapture/12/standalone_operator/field_verification_operator_work/)). *Takeoff:* every room fact (plotas, perimetras, aukštis, danga, durys/langai) carries a crop of the plan/eksplikacija/pjūvis cell it came from plus "Aukštis 2,70 m — pjūvis A-A, p. 4". Unhighlighted = unverifiable = needs a look (SymGen's convention).

2. **Confidence-gated auto-accept + a doubt queue ordered by money at stake.** Rossum auto-exports only fields above a per-field/per-queue threshold and routes the rest to review ([Rossum](https://knowledge-base.rossum.ai/docs/using-ai-confidence-thresholds-for-automation-in-rossum)); AWS A2I routes below-threshold or randomly sampled predictions ([AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/latest/dg/a2i-use-augmented-ai-a2i-human-review-loops.html)); V7 routes files to review when model/human IoU misses a threshold ([V7](https://docs.v7labs.com/docs/automate-your-review)). Cost-aware deferral cut error on retained cases 49.6% ([Sci Rep 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-40637-w)). Why money: a small set of cost-significant items carries ~80% of bill value ([Horner & Zakieh](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/014461996373494)); AI worklist reprioritization cut ICH wait 15.75→12.01 min/study ([Radiology: AI](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/full/10.1148/ryai.2020200024)). *Takeoff:* queue = doubt × (quantity × unit price); the dažomas plotas of the largest room comes first, a 0.4 m² WC door last.

3. **One keystroke = accept & next.** Rossum's screen: TAB walks all fields, ENTER jumps only to fields still needing a human ([Rossum](https://knowledge-base.rossum.ai/docs/data-capture-automation-with-rossum)); Encord: `n` approve / `b` reject ([Encord](https://docs.encord.com/platform-documentation/Annotate/annotate-label-editor/annotate-label-editor-review)); ABBYY: Enter confirms field ([ABBYY](https://help.abbyy.com/en-us/flexicapture/12/standalone_operator/field_verification_operator_work/)); Prodigy's binary interface exists to "fly through lots of inputs" ([Prodigy](https://prodi.gy/docs/api-interfaces)). Caveat: experienced users use shortcuts <10% of the time ([Lane et al. 2005](https://www.ruf.rice.edu/~lane/papers/hidden_costs.pdf)) — so Enter must be the *default* path, printed on the button. *Takeoff:* Enter = "Teisinga, kitas"; typing digits overwrites the value; Esc = "Nežinau, palikti".

4. **A correction shows its consequence immediately.** Lowering "the cost of validation by execution" mitigated over- and under-reliance on AI output (N=17, [Ferdowsi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.09541)); Rossum's built-in checks (base+tax=total etc.) block automation and flag the violated rule ([Rossum](https://knowledge-base.rossum.ai/docs/built-in-checks-overview)); guideline G16 "Convey the consequences of user actions" ([Amershi et al.](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf)). *Takeoff:* height 2,70→3,10 shows an inline diff: "dažomas plotas +14,2 m², glaistas +1 maišas, +312 €" and re-runs the sanity checks (walls 2.5–4× floor, baseboard ≈ perimeter − doors).

5. **Visible "corrected by you" state, with undo.** Encord colours pending/approved/rejected and fades reviewed labels ([Encord](https://docs.encord.com/platform-documentation/Annotate/annotate-label-editor/annotate-label-editor-review)); Label Studio records Accept vs Fix & Accept vs Reject ([HumanSignal](https://docs.humansignal.com/guide/quality)); Rossum's grey tick marks AI-validated fields ([Rossum](https://knowledge-base.rossum.ai/docs/data-capture-automation-with-rossum)); eTakeoff/Togal undo with Ctrl+Z ([eTakeoff](https://etakeoff.com/Help/TogalAI.html)). Undo/erase events surfaced >90% of severe usability problems (N=35, [Akers 2009](https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/2009/paper0569-akers-share.pdf)). *Takeoff:* badge "Jūsų pataisyta", original value on hover, Ctrl+Z; these rows are exactly what `build_ground_truth.py` harvests.

6. **Batch accept of identical things.** ABBYY group verification shows all characters recognised as the same value; Enter confirms the group, Space postpones one doubtful item ([ABBYY](https://help.abbyy.com/en-us/flexicapture/12/standalone_operator/group_verification_operator_work/)); Encord "Approve all in frame"; Scale Rapid's table view with checkbox bulk accept/reject ([Scale](https://scale.com/docs/rapid-or-auditing)). *Takeoff:* "6 durų 800×2000 — patvirtinti visas" with the six crops side by side; same for identical ceiling heights.

7. **Progress and what is left — front-loaded.** Break-off 21.8% when a bar started slow vs 11.3% when it started fast (no bar 12.7%) ([Conrad et al. 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2910434/)); a 32-experiment meta-analysis: constant bars don't help, slow-to-fast hurts ([Villar 2013](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/14427/)). *Takeoff:* count auto-accepted rooms as done from the start ("Liko 4 iš 23 · ~3 min"), never a bar that crawls.

8. **Direct manipulation on the drawing.** eTakeoff/Togal: click a detected measurement to pin/unpin it, Ctrl reveals same-class items ([eTakeoff](https://etakeoff.com/Help/TogalAI.html)); accepting/rejecting pre-drawn spans is 14–21% faster per entity than drawing them ([Lingren](https://academic.oup.com/jamia/article/21/3/406/2909253)). *Takeoff:* click a wall segment to toggle plytelės/dažai, click a door to change its type, drag a polygon vertex — while keeping a numeric field for typed overrides.

9. **Unambiguous displays: no bare percentages, no mixed units.** Natural frequencies tripled correct Bayesian inferences (16%→46%) ([Gigerenzer & Hoffrage](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.01473/full)); Microsoft explains 0.95 as "correct 19 out of 20 times" ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/document-intelligence/concept/accuracy-confidence?view=doc-intel-4.0.0)); confidence scores calibrated trust but did not by themselves improve decisions ([Zhang, Liao, Bellamy 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02114)); a valid-looking number in the wrong unit lost Mars Climate Orbiter ([summary](https://everydayastronaut.com/mars-climate-orbiter/)). *Takeoff:* say what disagrees ("plane 24,3 m², eksplikacijoje 25,1 m²"), unit on every number, one unit per column, "iki 3 m / virš 3 m" spelled out.

10. **What the system did NOT find = an explicit blank with a reason.** Telling humans the model deferred — without showing its guess — gave the strongest accuracy gains ([Bondi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06751)); Azure returns `NULL` with its own confidence ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/document-intelligence/concept/accuracy-confidence?view=doc-intel-4.0.0)); Rossum marks the blocking rule on the field ([Rossum](https://knowledge-base.rossum.ai/docs/built-in-checks-overview)); G2 "make clear how well the system can do" ([Amershi](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf)). *Takeoff:* "Aukštis: — (pjūvyje nerasta; įveskite arba priimkite 2,70 m)" rather than a 🔴 default that looks like data.

11. **Exception-only review plus random audit.** Reconciliation tools match automatically and route only exceptions ([Trintech](https://www.trintech.com/financial-process/transaction-matching/)); claims "auto-adjudication rate" is a standard KPI ([OpsDog](https://opsdog.com/products/claims-auto-adjudication-rate)); A2I supports random-sample review ([AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/latest/dg/a2i-use-augmented-ai-a2i-human-review-loops.html)) because automation bias makes experts skip verification of reliable automation ([Parasuraman & Manzey 2010](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0018720810376055)). *Takeoff:* show 🟡/🔴 facts plus a random 10% of 🟢 ones, unlabelled, and report the false-accept rate.

12. **Same-fact-type column sweep (spreadsheet-like).** ABBYY "Analogous fields" (Ctrl+Alt+F) shows the same field across documents ([ABBYY](https://help.abbyy.com/en-us/flexicapture/12/standalone_operator/field_verification_operator_work/)); Scale's table view exists "when comparing task responses" ([Scale](https://scale.com/docs/rapid-or-auditing)); Prodigy recommends one concept per pass ([Prodigy](https://prodi.gy/docs/text-classification)); annotators identify targets better when many instances share one view ([arXiv 2409.10272](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.10272v1)). *Takeoff:* a "Aukštis" column over all rooms makes the one 2,50 among 2,70s pop; sweep by fact type, not room by room.

## 2. Anti-patterns (slower than manual)

- **Review everything.** Google HITL's 100% threshold "will send all predicted documents for Human Review" ([Google](https://cloud.google.com/document-ai/docs/hitl/quickstart)); under multi-task load reviewers rubber-stamp ([Parasuraman & Manzey](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0018720810376055)).
- **Low-quality guesses shown as values.** Pre-annotation gave no significant time saving and lower quality than raw text in South 2014 ([JBI](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1532046414001191)); suggestions induced a small label bias in one domain ([Schulz](https://aclanthology.org/P19-1265.pdf)). Below threshold: blank + reason (pattern 10).
- **Context-less or tiny snippets.** ABBYY deliberately verifies characters "in the context of the whole field" and offers the page image with zoom ([ABBYY](https://help.abbyy.com/en-us/flexicapture/12/distributed_verificator/verificator_field_win/)); crops without neighbours remove the comparisons annotators rely on ([arXiv 2409.10272](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.10272v1)).
- **Hidden state changes after a correction** = "automation surprise" from silent mode changes ([Sarter & Woods](https://doi.org/10.1518/001872097778667997)); G18 "Notify users about changes" ([Amershi](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf)). Recompute must show the diff (pattern 4).
- **Multi-step corrections.** Google's HITL codelab needs hover→pencil→Apply→Confirm→Submit per entity ([Google](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/docai-hitl)); G9 "support efficient correction".
- **Priority queues without a floor.** AI-first ordering raised the *maximum* wait for critical cases and delayed normals 80→114 min ([Sci Rep](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8128725/)).
- **Raw confidence % as the primary signal** (Zhang 2020; Gigerenzer) — matches the 2026-09-04 decision: evidence first, no dots as signal.
- **Bulk actions that erase measurement.** Label Studio computes no lead time for bulk-labelled tasks ([HumanSignal](https://docs.humansignal.com/guide/labeling_bulk)); log batch accepts as their own event.
- **Undiscoverable shortcuts** ([Lane 2005](https://www.ruf.rice.edu/~lane/papers/hidden_costs.pdf)).

## 3. Pilot metrics

1. **Time to verified estimate vs manual.** Time each fact from card open to accept/correct, as Lingren timed seconds per entity (38–53 s → 29–45 s, p<0.01) and Schulz minutes per text; run the same contractor on one project with AI and one without (crossover). Stratify by workload — JACR found the gain only in busy hours. Label Studio's "lead time" (avg/median/total per annotator) is the template ([HumanSignal](https://docs.humansignal.com/guide/quality)).
2. **Acceptance rate**, split Accepted / Fixed-then-accepted / Rejected (Label Studio trichotomy); acceptance rate was the best predictor of perceived productivity for Copilot ([Ziegler et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06537)). Report the touchless share (Rossum's "automation" framing).
3. **Correction rate per fact type and per confidence bucket** (area, perimeter, height, finish, doors, windows, flooring): Instabase benchmarks per-field accuracy against ground truth ([Instabase](https://www.instabase.com/blog/ai-hub-october-update-manage-review-queues-benchmark-accuracy-and-easily-chat-across-multiple-documents)); bucket corrections by 🟢/🟡/🔴 to test calibration, since Rossum says thresholds must be validated on your data.
4. **False-accept rate**: re-check a random 10% of auto-accepted rows (A2I-style sampling); bias test à la Lingren — agreement with an independent manual pass with vs without suggestions (their IAA stayed 93.4–95.5%).
5. **€ at risk caught per review minute**: sum |Δquantity × price| of corrections ÷ time; expect Horner-Zakieh concentration.
6. **Undo/backtrack count per session** as a usability alarm ([Akers](https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/2009/paper0569-akers-share.pdf)); **max age of an unreviewed item** (tail guard).
