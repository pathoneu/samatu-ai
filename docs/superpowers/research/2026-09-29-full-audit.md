## Verdict

1. **The engineering is solid where it has been measured.** Backend 3799 tests pass, frontend 771 pass, lint is clean. The arithmetic core is deterministic: recomputing a project twice changes 0 of 116 rows on objektas_h. Every row's ledger replays to its quantity. The July security work holds: the security lane found no cross-tenant access hole.
2. **Three defects can put a wrong euro figure in front of a client today.** A Q4 putty choice is silently reverted to Q2 by the next room edit (calc-engine-1). The contractor-template export prices a summed cell at the first row's price (exports-money-1). The 21 % PVM line disappears when the model labels a project "renovacija" (exports-money-2).
3. **Accuracy numbers flatter the product.** Totals read 9.47 % in-sample and 7.06 % on objektas_g/h, but the contractor's own rows within 10 % are 27.5 % on the corpus (G 15 %, H 29.2 %). G and H have been tuned on for three weeks, so they are no longer out of sample (testing-eval-1). The eval exit code is always 1, so it gates nothing (testing-eval-4).
4. **Drawing-read values are overwritten by weaker guesses that keep the "read" label.** The paid zoom pass replaces heights without checking their source, and other lanes have the same pattern (vision-pipeline-2, vision-pipeline-3, geometry-lanes-1, geometry-lanes-5).
5. **Rows are fabricated and shown as read.** Every bathroom gets a shower; sanitary rows are room-type defaults; a door seen in one of three voting rounds is kept; LED niche length is inferred statistically (calc-engine-6, vision-pipeline-1, vision-pipeline-5, vision-pipeline-7).
6. **Spend control is broken in both directions.** Caps default to off with open sign-up. Failed runs are not metered. Free editing endpoints are charged a phantom 0.15 EUR each (693.80 EUR phantom against 18.52 EUR real in the local ledger), so switching the cap on would lock users out of editing.
7. **The largest risk is not in the code.** No external project since 2026-08-03, every truth file comes from one contractor, production still runs the build rejected on 2026-09-17, and the public copy promises minutes and six construction types.

### Drawing reading with Vision

**What is good, leave it alone**

- Pass-1 call (_run_pass1_once, vision_call.py:4803-4945) handles refusal and max_tokens, filters thinking blocks by type, and has a one-shot document-to-raster fallback that cannot loop. extractor/response_text.py is used by every extractor reader; only two legacy main.py helpers still read content[0].text.
- apdailos_reader.py:356-440 is the model reader: bounded retries with a retryable-error test, refusal handled without retry, a truncated-but-salvaged read reported as 'partial' with a visible LT warning, tokens metered on every path. Copy it rather than rewrite it.
- The deterministic €0 lanes are the right architecture for 'vector geometry for WHERE, Vision for WHAT': pdfplumber eksplikacija with the foreign-table guard and re-read from the rooms' own pages, plan-area labels, dimension strings, wall outlines, positioned height markers, vector symbol counts, door schedule. The findings here are about later steps overwriting them, not about the lanes.
- Cost capture is careful: every sub-reader's tokens land in _meta.extra_calls priced at its own model's rate, an unknown model raises instead of pricing silently, and per-round cache telemetry exists. That telemetry is what exposed the cache-TTL and truncation problems.
- Joining eksplikacija rows by number first, with a name-contradiction guard and unique-name fallback (vision_call.py:5530-5560), fixed a real duplicate-name bug and is deterministic.
- Stream disconnect survival (main.py _drain_gen/_save_orphaned_result) and fail-open page selection with recorded reasons are solid operational design.

**Open questions from this lane**

- Should statistical LED-niche inference exist at all? It rests on Objektas A's sąmata ratios, and on objektas_g it produced 70 % of the billed LED metres against a contractor sąmata with no LED row. Or should an unread LED niche become a blank row that asks for the length?
- Are multi-storey houses in scope for launch? No corpus PDF has more than one plan sheet, and the doors reader, the global height-marker pick and `aukstas` (never filled) all assume one storey.
- Budget one paid A/B on the fixtures? effort='high' vs 'medium' (or a thinking budget) for pass-1, and a larger max_tokens. Thinking is ~70 % of pass-1 output tokens (~$1–1.4/project) and already truncates rounds.
- Opus 5.5 ($4/$20, cache reads 0.05×) is priced in pricing.py but has never been run on LT drawings. Worth a bake-off, given pass-1 is 50–70 % of a €2–4.3 project?
- Keep the Vision door-type reader (3 rounds, ~$0.33) now that door_schedule.py settles frame type deterministically on most corpus projects, or run it only when the €0 scan finds nothing?
- Do non-LT drawings (English or Russian labels) matter for the first customers? The pdfplumber eksplikacija parser and plan lanes are LT-vocabulary-gated, so those projects fall back to Vision end to end.
- OK to turn off the disk VisionCache for production extractions? It currently makes re-running an extraction unable to fix a bad adjacency or niche read.
