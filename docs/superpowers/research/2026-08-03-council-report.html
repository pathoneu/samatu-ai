The one thing to do first
Verify R2 on Railway — 10 minutes. The only item whose failure mode is "the contractor's PDF and project are unrecoverable", and it gates the consent answer you owe him before he uploads a client's drawings. curl /api/admin/v2/system-health → storage_backend == "r2"

Where the council agrees

  • corrections = 0 is the central fact, and the confidence bug caused it. 164/273 engine-flagged rows render green, the queue shows zero, the app says "visi duomenys patvirtinti" — no user was ever told there is anything to correct. The flywheel isn't unused; it's severed.
  • The pre-contractor cut is app-side only: confidence fix (with € framing), kaina_source, LT error messages, zero-room salvage, R2 check, done-notification. No model work, no truth edits.
  • Gemini probe: not now. A ≥2-pt gate inside a ±2-pt noise band decides nothing. Revisit at n≥15 human-verified rows; keep the provider-swappable env as pricing leverage.
  • Truth-file fixes wait — truth-only commit, before/after in the message, after the contractor.
  • The test is a data-acquisition event: ≥20 correction rows, session recorded, and he brings one already-priced project (free human n=5). Sit beside him.

Full advisor responses

The Contrarian — "You are optimizing a demo you should not be running live"

The fatal flaw: you are optimizing a demo you should not be running live. 27 min → 13 min is still a dead room. Pre-run the contractor's PDF the night before, on the current code, at €2.53. That deletes speed from the pre-contractor cut entirely — cache, parallel voting, zoom parallelization, all of it. "Zero-accuracy-risk by construction" is an argument, not a measurement; parallel voting already had one race, and races surface on an unfamiliar PDF at 3pm, not in 8 tests. Ship them after.

The confidence fix is a demo risk, not a demo win. Today the app lies pleasantly. Fixed, it tells a first-time contractor that 164 of 273 rows are unverified. Ship it only with the €-ranked queue framing attached and rehearsed — the fix without the framing makes retention worse.

Truth-file edit: not now, and be suspicious of it. The engine's author declaring the truth file wrong, on n=4, moving the corpus 9.31→5.19, is the textbook way to fool yourself. The contractor is a better arbiter than your own derivation. Label it, park it. Gemini probe: never, as specified — a ≥2pt gate inside a ±2pt noise band cannot distinguish a win from nothing.

What the test must measure, and doesn't: you have no persisted error log, no alerting, no restore path. If his run fails you'll have poppler stderr and a shrug. Biggest omission: no story for being wrong. His PDF is out-of-distribution from your four; 9.31% predicts nothing about it. The correction workspace shouldn't be a feature he might find — it should be the thing you sit beside him and use.

First Principles — "You have no oracle, and everything downstream is theater"

n=4. Truth files you now know are wrong. A metric that moves 4 points when you fix bookkeeping instead of the product. Corrections table: 0 rows, ever. You are not measuring accuracy — you are measuring agreement between Claude and a founder's earlier reading of the same PDF.

The load-bearing insight: the corrections table is empty not because of low usage — because the app tells every user there is nothing to correct. You built a feedback loop and then shipped a bug that severs it. That bug is not a P0 UI defect. It is the reason your entire ground-truth strategy has produced nothing in months. The confidence-predicate fix and the kaina_source fix are the whole pre-contractor cut.

Truth files — don't "correct" them, retire them; contractor-verified numbers supersede them within days. Gemini — never on n=4; revisit at n≥15 human-verified. 27 minutes isn't the problem; 27 unexplained minutes is. Biggest miss: no telemetry on the one experiment that matters — run this test blind and you spend a real estimator's attention to learn a vibe.

The Expansionist — "Honest uncertainty is the wedge; the test is data acquisition"

The corrections table reading 0 is the only number on this page that matters. Reframe the confidence bug as the flagship feature: a €-ranked review queue that says these 12 rows carry €40k of your bid, check these three is something Togal, Kreo and Bluebeam do not ship. Nobody else can honestly price uncertainty because nobody else knows where their numbers came from.

Make the contractor test a data-acquisition event: he brings one finished project with his own final quantities — n=4→n=5 with human truth, free, the first non-AI-seeded fixture you'll ever own. Record the session. Measure corrections written, minutes to first correction, which rows he distrusts before opening them.

Speed is not comfort, it's headroom — permission to spend more compute per run. And the kainynas is a second moat: accumulate prices per contractor and it becomes a Lithuanian price benchmark nobody can replicate.

The Outsider — "27 and 13 minutes are the same event; the ping matters"

Nothing in the speed workstream matters to the person arriving — "leave, come back later" is the same event at 27 or 13 minutes. What breaks it is the missing done-notification; that's P0, not P1. The loudest fact in the brief: the app says "visi duomenys patvirtinti" while 164 of 273 rows are flagged — the product asserting a falsehood about a number the contractor will sign his name under. And silently eating hand-typed prices is data destruction in front of a customer.

330 pointer actions for 82 rows is where he quits; he can type prices into Excel faster. Watch where he stops scrolling, which number he reaches for first, whether he finishes. Ask him to bring a job he already priced — free ground truth. Biggest miss: you wrote "first impressions decide retention," then built a roadmap of work he will never see. Also — nobody has said what he pays.

Be on the call.
