# LLM Council — 2-Week Roadmap Verdict (2026-08-03)

Process: 5 advisors (Contrarian, First Principles, Expansionist, Outsider, Executor — all Opus 5) answered independently; anonymized peer review (3 reviewers — stopped at 3, not the skill's 5, because their "strongest" picks already split three ways and blind-spot themes repeated; disclosed deliberately); chairman synthesis by Fable 5 (director).

## Chairman's Verdict

### Where the council agrees (independent convergence — high confidence)

1. **The corrections table at 0 rows is the central fact of the company**, and the confidence-vocabulary bug is *causally upstream* of it: the app renders 164/273 engine-flagged rows green, empties the review queue, and asserts "visi duomenys patvirtinti" — so no user has ever been told there is anything to correct. The flywheel isn't unused; it's severed. (B's causal chain, endorsed by all three reviewers.)
2. **The pre-contractor cut is small and entirely app-side.** Confidence fix (with €-ranked framing), kaina_source, LT error messages, zero-room salvage, R2 verification, done-notification. No model work, no truth edits, no new speed features beyond what's already validated.
3. **Gemini symbol probe: not now.** The pre-registered gate (≥2pts on n=4) sits inside the corpus's own ±2pt noise band — the rule cannot distinguish a win from nothing (D and B independently). Revisit at n≥15 human-verified rows. Keep `SAMATU_MODEL_SYMBOLS` provider-swappability — it's pricing leverage.
4. **Truth-file corrections: park until after the contractor.** Fix later in a truth-only commit with before/after in the message. The contractor is a better arbiter than the founder's re-derivation.
5. **The contractor test is a data-acquisition event, not a demo.** Success metric = corrections written (target ≥20 rows), session recorded, and he brings one *already-priced finished project* — free human ground truth, the first non-AI-seeded fixture ever.
6. **Sit beside him.** The correction workspace must be used together, not discovered.

### Where the council clashes

- **Speed before the test.** Contrarian: pre-run his PDF the night before and delete speed from the cut entirely; races surface on unfamiliar PDFs, not in tests. Executor/Expansionist: validate the parse cache before (it must be validated eventually; ~€2.50; 27→~20 min). Outsider: 27 vs 13 min is the same human event — the notification is what matters. **Resolution: all three.** Pre-run his PDF the night before (insurance + a second artifact to correct in the room without a 27-min wait); enable the parse cache only, after one validation run; leave parallel voting and zoom/reader parallelization OFF until after (sub-readers have no timeout/retry — parallelizing that ships a hang).
- **Confidence fix: risk or headline?** Contrarian: telling a first-timer "164 rows unverified" churns him unless the framing ships and is rehearsed. Expansionist: the €-ranked honest-uncertainty queue is a category differentiator no competitor ships. **Resolution: both are right — the framing IS the feature.** Ship only with €-impact ranking + `_confidence_reason` displayed; rehearse the walkthrough.
- **What the session optimizes: trust or data.** "He finishes and comes back" and "he writes 20 corrections" pull opposite ways (caught by reviewers, named by no advisor). **Resolution: sequence it.** First half: his flow, untouched, on his fresh PDF. Second half: guided correction pass on the pre-run project, top-€ rows first.

### Blind spots the peer-review round caught

1. **Truth-laundering hazard:** correcting truth files "through the correction workspace" (E's idea) would launder the founder's own re-derivation into rows `build_ground_truth` treats as human-confirmed. Only the contractor's hands write corrections.
2. **Privacy/consent:** he hands over a real client's priced project — into a system with an unverified R2 backup and a prior client-name-leak history (fixture scrub, 2026-07). Before the session: explicit consent, retention/deletion answer, R2 verified.
3. **n=1 is not an oracle either.** Two LT estimators routinely differ 10–15%. His numbers are a better prior than AI-seeded truth, not gospel; the corpus eventually needs multiple humans.
4. **No re-run story in the room.** A correction round costs another 27 min / €2.53 — the night-before pre-run is what makes live iteration possible at all.
5. **Nobody's job is watching the run live.** Founder on the call, `railway logs` tailed, no pushes that day (`safe_deploy_check.sh`).
6. **Nobody has said what he pays** (Outsider). Have the price conversation at the test; billing already exists behind the flag.

### The recommendation

**Phase 0 — before the contractor (~2–3 founder-days):**
- Day 1 = merge train, no new code: Prielaidos idempotency, tile 27+28, PATALPOS labels, height-marker boundary fix, PDF-parse cache, plus the two running fix sessions (confidence+framing, kaina_source) as they complete. R2 env verification + healthcheck→/health *first thing* (10 min). Gates once at end of day.
- Day 2: LED inference gate (−1.5 pts, simulated, bit-identical elsewhere); LT PDF error messages (30 min); zero-room salvage (1–2h); ONE €2.50 validation run with cache enabled; parallel voting stays off.
- Day 3: price-entry editor port (~3h — 330 pointer actions → ~82 keystrokes); done-notification + title flip (~1h); fix the "2–20 min" copy; rehearse the demo end-to-end once.
**Phase 1 — the test itself:** night-before pre-run of his PDF; consent + retention said out loud; sit beside him; his-flow-first then guided corrections; target ≥20 correction rows; record; ask for one finished priced project (n=5, human); talk price.
**Phase 2 — after (week 2):** enable remaining speed levers one at a time behind flags with a measured run each (voting-parallel warm → zoom → sub-readers, after adding timeouts/retries); truth-file corrections in a labelled truth-only commit; foreign-project room-key matching; sanity-warning surfacing; save-prices-as-kainynas (the second moat); error-log persistence + restore rehearsal; Gemini decision deferred to n≥15.
**Rejected:** VOTING_N=2 (structurally broken), mixed-provider voting, cheap-model page classification (no caller), full Gemini migration, any accuracy tuning against `derived`-confidence truth metrics.

### The one thing to do first

**Verify R2 on Railway (10 minutes).** It is the only item on the entire board whose failure mode is "the contractor's PDF and project are unrecoverable," it gates the privacy answer you owe him before he uploads a client's drawings, and it is invisible until the exact moment it's catastrophic. `curl /api/admin/v2/system-health` → `storage_backend` must say `"r2"`.
