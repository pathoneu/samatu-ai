# Council Transcript — 2026-05-08

**Topic:** How to improve AI accuracy on PDF analysis for Sąmatų AI (Lithuanian construction takeoff app, beta).

---

## Original question

> consult the council. My app is now in beta testing. Check and review the code, understand how the app works, and tell me how can we improve on AI analyzing projects/pdf so we have more calculation accuracy.

## Framed question (delivered to all 5 advisors)

Sąmatų AI is a Lithuanian construction takeoff app in beta. It uses Claude Opus 4.7 vision to read construction PDFs and extract quantities (areas, perimeters, ceiling heights, window/door counts), then deterministic Python applies LT-specific formulas (paint, drywall, tiles, baseboard, hidroizoliacija) and runs 24 sanity/cross-page validation rules.

**Current pipeline:** Pass 1 = single Opus call across all pages at 200 DPI extracting room "primitives" only (Vision is explicitly told NOT to calculate). Pass 2 = per-room zoom call at 300 DPI to refine mm-level dimensions. Page filtering (vendor quotes) and ceiling-height fallback (text scan for `+2.800` markers) are deterministic.

**Bake-off results (Apr 2026):** Of 6 pipelines tested, P1 (Claude Vision direct) won at 49.3% accuracy, $0.10/page, 171s. P3 template matching over-counted 100×, P5 GroundingDINO ran out of credit, P6 Claude tool-orchestration was 23.3% accuracy at 12× cost. **CRITICAL CAVEAT:** ground truth is AI-seeded — measured against Claude, not human-verified. `build_ground_truth.py` is planned but doesn't exist yet.

**Known accuracy weaknesses identified by code audit:**
1. `scale_validator.py` is dormant — Vision detects scale text but never populates `diagonal_px` for cross-checking, so 4× systematic scale errors go undetected.
2. Pass 2 zoom doesn't receive Pass 1 confidence/warnings — no feedback loop between calls.
3. Every room gets zoom-read regardless of confidence (latency cost; no adaptive routing).
4. Handwritten/scanned annotations not special-cased.
5. Vision prompting: page label not consistently placed BEFORE each image, `output_config.format` JSON schema not used (still relying on regex repair fallback), `bbox_pct` field still in schema (wastes attention).
6. `learn_from_corrections.py` exists but corrections never auto-feed back into the system prompt.
7. Deterministic-formula bugs: default window area uses 1.40 m² (US) instead of LT STR 1.80 m² (22% undercount); reveals computed for 3 sides instead of 4 (29% undercount); grindjuostės double-counts shared interior walls (+88% over-estimate vs rangovas baseline).
8. Hidden positions missing: "Angokraščių apdirbimas GKP" (~€1,410/project), tile microwork (~€1,300/project), paslėptų durų staktu apdirbimas (~€2,000/project).

**Stage:** Beta testers using it now. Stack locked (react-pdf + Konva). No human-confirmed ground truth exists.

**The question:** How should they improve AI accuracy on PDF analysis? Where's the biggest leverage — better prompting, multi-pass with feedback, fixing deterministic formula bugs, building human ground truth, or rethinking the architecture? What are they wrong about?

## Advisor responses (full)

### The Contrarian (Response C)

You are measuring a vision pipeline against itself and calling the result "49.3% accuracy." That number is fiction. You don't know if you're at 30% or 70%. Every optimization decision you've made — picking P1, killing P3, ranking P6 — rests on a benchmark whose ground truth was generated by the same model family you're scoring. You could be reinforcing Claude's systematic biases and calling it progress.

Worse: you have **beta users right now** producing real estimates from a 49%-against-itself system, with known formula bugs that undercount paint reveals by 29% and overcount baseboard by 88%. A contractor will bid a job using your numbers, lose money, and the story isn't "beta software" — it's "AI tool gave wrong quote." That's your reputation, not a bug ticket.

The biggest leverage isn't prompting or architecture. It's this brutal sequence:

1. **Stop shipping accuracy claims today.** The UI says "49.3%" somewhere implicitly. Replace it with "AI draft — verify every number." Honest disclosure beats clever fixes — your own CLAUDE.md says this.
2. **Fix the deterministic bugs this week.** Window 1.40→1.80, reveals 3→4 sides, grindjuostės shared-wall dedup, hidden positions. These are *known wrong math*, not accuracy mysteries. No ground truth needed. Every day you don't fix them, beta users get worse numbers from confidently-presented formulas.
3. **Then build ground truth.** 20 PDFs, human-confirmed, before any more pipeline work. Without it, you're doing astrology.

What you're wrong about: you're treating Vision accuracy as the bottleneck. It isn't. Your bottleneck is that **the deterministic layer downstream of Vision has bugs that dwarf the Vision error bars**, and you've been optimizing the noisy stage while the clean stage silently corrupts outputs. Fix the math first. It's free.

### The First Principles Thinker (Response A)

You're asking the wrong question.

"How do we improve AI accuracy?" assumes accuracy is the product. It isn't. The product is **a quantity schedule a contractor will stake money on.** Those are different things, and conflating them is why you're stuck.

Strip it down. What's the actual job? A samatininkas takes 8 hours, produces a KŽ, and is wrong by ~10–15% — and contractors price-in that error with margin. You're at 49.3% measured against *yourself*. You don't have an accuracy problem. You have an **epistemology problem**: you literally cannot tell if you're improving.

Every item on your list — better prompting, multi-pass feedback, formula bugs, architecture — is downstream of one fact: **you have no ground truth.** Optimizing a pipeline against AI-seeded labels is optimizing Claude's agreement with itself. You could "improve" to 80% and have shipped nothing.

So the leverage isn't in the list you gave. It's:

1. **Build ground truth this week.** Not the 1-hour interactive flow — sit with one real samatininkas for a day on 3 real PDFs. Everything else is theater until this exists.
2. **Fix the deterministic bugs immediately.** Item 7 isn't an "AI accuracy" issue — it's wrong arithmetic shipping to users right now. 22% + 29% + 88% errors are not in the same category as "Vision misread a number." Stop calling them the same problem.
3. **Then ask: do contractors need 95% accuracy, or 70% accuracy + a 10-minute correction UI?** Your own bake-off doc already answered this. You're ignoring your own conclusion.

You're not wrong about the techniques. You're wrong about the order.

## Chairman synthesis

### Where the council agrees (4 of 5 advisors converged independently)

1. **The 49.3% accuracy number is meaningless.** The bake-off ground truth is AI-seeded — Claude grading Claude. You cannot tell if you're at 30%, 50%, or 70%. Every optimization decision downstream of that benchmark is suspect.
2. **The biggest accuracy lever is NOT the Vision pipeline.** It's the deterministic Python downstream of Vision: window default 1.40 → 1.80 m² (22% undercount), reveals 3-side → 4-side (29% undercount), grindjuostės shared-wall dedup (88% overshoot), and the €4,700/project of missing line items. These are wrong arithmetic, not AI accuracy. They are free to fix and require no ground truth.
3. **Build human ground truth before any further Vision tuning.** 1 day with 1 samatininkas on 3–5 PDFs. Without this, prompt fixes, scale validation, Pass-2 feedback loops, and adaptive zoom are all "optimizing against noise" (D) or "doing astrology" (C).
4. **The product is a contractor-stake-able schedule, not a pretty extraction.** Estimators verify everything regardless of model accuracy. Correction speed beats absolute accuracy.

### Where the council clashes

**Strategic posture (E vs A/B/C/D):** E uniquely argues "stop optimizing the detector — pivot to corrections-flywheel + classification API + forensic-audit product." All four other advisors say "you have a beta with broken math; fix the math first." All five reviewers flagged E's framing as the biggest blind spot of the round. The peer review verdict is unanimous: E is right that there's strategic upside in the corrections flywheel, but wrong on timing — you can't license a dataset built on a broken Python layer, and you can't pivot positioning while live users are bidding wrong numbers.

**Sequencing (A's "ground truth first" vs D's "constants first"):** A says epistemology before arithmetic. D says deploy the constants in 2 hours and ground truth on Tuesday. Reasonable advisors disagree because the answer depends on duration: D's order works because constants are 2 hours of work and ground truth is 1 day; reversing them costs nothing. A's framing matters because it disciplines what comes *after* — without ground truth, no later optimization is honest.

**Correction UI vs detector improvements (B/E vs A/C/D):** B and E say the correction UI is the product, not the Vision pipeline. A, C, D treat it as a later step (correctly noting it's premature without first fixing the underlying math). The clash is genuine: ship correction UI first to make 50% accuracy livable, OR fix the math first so correction UI doesn't paper over silent bugs. The peer reviews favor "fix math first" — fixing the formulas is hours of work, the UI is weeks.

### Blind spots the council caught (peer review revealed, unanimous 5/5)

**Liability and disclosure to existing beta users.** This is the single largest gap in the round, flagged by every peer reviewer. Live samatininkai are right now signing contracts using outputs with known errors on specific line items (windows, reveals, grindjuostės). None of the five advisors proposed:

- Notifying current beta users that past estimates may need re-checking on those specific positions.
- Adding a banner tied to the known-buggy formulas (e.g. "Žinomos formulių klaidos — prieš pateikiant pasiūlymą patikrinkite langų plotus, angokraščius ir grindjuostes").
- Gating or rolling back the buggy outputs until the Hour-1 fixes are deployed.

This is not a roadmap question. It's an ethical and legal exposure question. CLAUDE.md says "honest disclosure beats clever fixes" — that principle extends backward to estimates already in the wild, not just forward to future runs.

**E's blind spot specifically:** E doesn't mention fixing the deterministic bugs at all. The advisor pivots straight to dataset/API/forensic-audit positioning without acknowledging that the foundation those rest on is currently broken. Reviewer 3: "You can't license a human-verified LT dataset built on top of a system that double-counts shared walls."

### The recommendation

**Stop chasing AI accuracy improvements. Vision is not your problem.**

The bake-off, the prompt tuning, the scale_validator activation, the Pass-2 feedback loop, the adaptive zoom — all of it is downstream of two facts the council surfaced:

- The deterministic Python layer has formula bugs (22% to 88% errors on specific positions) that dwarf any plausible Vision improvement.
- You have no ground truth, so you cannot measure whether a Vision change helps or hurts.

Execute in this order:

**Today (4 hours of work + 1 deploy):**
- Ship a "AI juodraštis — patikrinkite kiekius" banner on every estimate. Name the known-buggy positions explicitly: langų plotai, angokraščiai, grindjuostės, paslėptų durų staktos. Email beta users with the same disclosure.
- Push the four constants: window default 1.40 → 1.80 m²; reveal formula 3-side → 4-side; grindjuostės empirical cap (the 2-hour empirical version, not the 12-hour polygon-graph version); add the three missing line items (Angokraščių apdirbimas GKP, paslėptų durų staktos, six tile microwork items). This recovers ~€4,700/project.

**This week (1 day of work):**
- Sit with one real samatininkas for a full day on 3 real PDFs. Manually verify every quantity. This is `build_ground_truth.py` reframed as "1 day of human work, not an interactive tool to build later." Re-run the 6-pipeline bake-off against this ground truth. The new accuracy number is the only honest one you'll have ever seen.

**Next two weeks:**
- Vision prompt fixes (image-label-before-image, output_config.format JSON schema, remove bbox_pct, ensure effort=high) — measured against the new ground truth.
- Activate scale_validator (Vision must populate diagonal_px). This catches 4× systematic scale errors.

**Defer (do not start now):**
- Auto-correction-loop from user overrides. Research project; no clear schema. Punt 6 weeks.
- Pass-2 feedback loop and adaptive zoom. Optimizing against noise without ground truth.
- Phase C-3 flood-fill polygon. 2 weeks for a feature that doesn't move the accuracy number.
- Streaming SSE, page-class chips, bidirectional canvas↔list. UX polish — important but not accuracy work.
- E's three platform plays (dataset business, classification API, forensic-audit product). Compelling but premature. Revisit only after the math is correct AND you have 60+ days of real human-verified corrections logged.

The chairman sides with D's sequencing and C's urgency framing. A's "epistemology first" is philosophically right but operationally subsumed: D's order (constants in hours, ground truth on Tuesday) honors A's principle without delaying the free arithmetic wins.
